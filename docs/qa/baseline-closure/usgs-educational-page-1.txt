Perseverance Rover Landing Site 
 
USGS Astrogeology Scientists helped create this map of Jezero crater, the landing site for NASA’s Perseverance 
rover. The oval or “ellipse” on the map is the area where the rover is most likely to touch down when it lands. Color 
the landing site by number and help the mission team land the rover and discover new rocks and sediments at 
Perseverance’s new home.
